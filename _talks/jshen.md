---
title: Subgroup zeta functions of higher Heisenberg groups
speaker: Jianhao Shen
affiliation: Bielefeld University
abstract: |
  Subgroup zeta functions encode subgroup growth in finitely
  generated nilpotent groups, but are difficult to compute explicitly,
  even for one of the simplest families of nonabelian nilpotent groups:
  the higher Heisenberg groups. I will discuss explicit formulae for these
  groups, based on recent joint work with C. Voll.
