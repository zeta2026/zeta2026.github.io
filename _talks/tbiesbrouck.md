---
title: Birational Zeta Functions
speaker: Tom Biesbrouck
affiliation: KU Leuven
abstract: |
  The motivic zeta function is a classical rich invariant in singularity
  theory. In this talk, I will report on joint work with N. Budur, J. Nicaise
  and W. Veys where we define a birational analog of the motivic zeta function
  of a reduced polynomial f in terms of minimal models. It admits an intrinsic
  meaning in terms of contact loci of arcs, an analog of a result of Denef and
  Loeser in the motivic case. We show that for local plane curve singularities
  the poles of the birational zeta function essentially coincide with the
  poles of the motivic zeta function.
