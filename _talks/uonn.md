---
title: On Representation Zeta Functions in Positive Characteristic 
speaker: Uri Onn
affiliation: Australian National University
abstract: |
  Representation zeta functions are Dirichlet generating functions associated
  with the representation growth of groups, encoding the numbers of
  irreducible representations of each dimension. The representation zeta
  functions of $p$-adic and arithmetic groups in characteristic zero have been
  studied extensively over the past two decades. By contrast, the
  positive-characteristic case remains comparatively unexplored, owing in part
  to the absence of the orbit method and the limited availability of
  resolution-of-singularities techniques. In this lecture, I will survey some
  of the known results in this area and present recent joint work with
  Amritanshu Prasad and Pooja Singla on the representation zeta functions of
  groups of type $A_2$.
