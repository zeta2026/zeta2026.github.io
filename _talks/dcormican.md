---
title: Ask Zeta Functions of Unitary Lie Algebras
speaker: David Cormican
affiliation: University of Galway
abstract: |
  Let $K$ be a quadratic algebraic number field and $\mathfrak{O}_K$ its ring
  of integers. For prime $p$ inert in $O_K$ and almost all $p$ which split in
  $O_K$, we present recent work giving the ask zeta function of
  $\mathfrak{gu}_d(\mathfrak{O}_K/\mathbb{Z}) \otimes \mathbb{Z}_p$ and
  $\mathfrak{su}_d(\mathfrak{O}_K/\mathbb{Z}) \otimes \mathbb{Z}_p$ for the
  general and special unitary Lie algebras of dimension $d > 1$ over
  $\mathfrak{O}_K$. We note that these local ask zeta functions are identical,
  except in the case of the $d = 2$ where the prime $p$ splits. We will also
  explain the links between this work and earlier investigations of ask
  squared zeta functions for $\mathfrak{gl}_d(\mathbb{Z}_p)$ and conjugacy
  class zeta functions for certain unipotent group schemes.
  
sort_key: "Cormican, David"
